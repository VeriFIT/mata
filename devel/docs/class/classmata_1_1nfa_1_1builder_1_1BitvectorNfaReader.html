Class mata::nfa::builder::BitvectorNfaReader¶

class BitvectorNfaReader¶

Reads NFAs with bitvector alphabets (@NFA-bits) over one shared set of minterms, as the general reader followed by Mintermization::mintermize() of all of them together does, but without the intermediate representations.

Transitions whose formulas are the usual Boolean combinations of symbols are read directly into BDDs. Any other @NFA-bits input goes through the general reader inside add(), so that all added automata still share one partition. The NFAs are the ones parse_from_mata() has always returned, except that the initial and the listed final states are numbered in the order of the input.

Read add(std::string_view nfa_in_mata, bool keep_quotes = false, NameStateMap *state_map = nullptr)¶

Reads nfa_in_mata, an input with a single NFA section with a bitvector alphabet.

Parameters:

  • keep_quotes – Whether quoted names of body lines keep their quotes, see mata::parser::parse_mf().

  • state_map – If not nullptr, the map from state names to states of this automaton, see construct(). build() reads and fills it, so it has to outlive the call of build().

Throws:

std::runtime_error – A bitvector NFA the general reader rejects; nothing of it is kept.

std::vector<Nfa> build(Alphabet *alphabet = nullptr)¶

Builds the NFAs of all the inputs add() kept, in the order they were added.

Their symbols are the minterms of the predicates of all transitions of all of them, named by their numbers and translated by alphabet.

Parameters:

alphabet – Alphabet translating the minterm names; nullptr reads them as numbers (IntAlphabet).
