Class mata::NameStore¶

class NameStore¶

An append-only storage of names: the tokens of a parsed section and the formula nodes of an intermediate automaton keep views into it instead of strings of their own.

The bytes handed out are never moved, so a view stays valid for as long as the storage lives. Copies of parser::ParsedSection and of IntermediateAut share the storage, which keeps the views of the copies valid.

Public Functions

inline std::string_view store(const std::string_view name)¶

Copies name into the storage.

Threads may store concurrently; each view points to its own copy.

Returns:

A view of the copy, valid for as long as the storage lives.
