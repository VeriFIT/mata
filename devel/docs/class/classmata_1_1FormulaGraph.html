Class mata::FormulaGraph¶

class FormulaGraph¶

A transition formula as a tree whose nodes are the operators and operands of the formula.

E.g., a formula q1 & s1 is a tree with & as the root node and q1 and s1 as the children of the root. The nodes are stored in one vector in postfix order, so a whole formula takes a single allocation. A negation has one child, a conjunction and a disjunction two, and any other node none.
