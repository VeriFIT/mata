Struct mata::nfa::PostWorkspace¶

struct PostWorkspace

Reusable scratch buffers of the successor and epsilon-closure computations.

The buffers only ever hold states the call reaches, so a call costs time proportional to what it visits, never to the number of states of the automaton. A fresh workspace per call is therefore cheap; reusing one across calls also saves the buffer allocations.

A workspace belongs to one call or to one explicit caller context. It must never be shared between threads, and in particular it must never be a function-local static: that is the data race of #731.
