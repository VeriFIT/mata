Struct mata::parser::Re2Options¶

struct Re2Options

Options of the conversion of a regex to an NFA and of the post-processing of the result.

The defaults reproduce the behaviour of the overloads taking the individual parameters: epsilon transitions removed, the result trimmed and reduced by simulation reduction.

Public Members

bool keep_epsilon = {false}

Translate every instruction of the regex program to a state, with an epsilon transition for each step that consumes no symbol. Otherwise, the regex is converted directly to an NFA without epsilon transitions.

Symbol epsilon = {306}

Symbol representing epsilon transitions.

Encoding encoding = {Encoding::Latin1}

Encoding of the regex. Trim the created NFA. Without epsilon transitions, the conversion only creates states reachable from the initial state, and only a branch that can never match, such as ‘[^\x00-\xff]’ in Latin1, leaves useless states behind.

bool reduce = {true}

Reduce the created NFA by simulation reduction. The reduction costs seconds on regexes with a few thousand states, where the conversion itself takes milliseconds; turn it off for large regexes and when the result is determinized anyway.
