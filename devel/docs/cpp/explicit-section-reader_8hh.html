Detailed Description

Fast reader of the .mata sections whose lines are plain lists of names.

The general reader, parse_mf() followed by IntermediateAut::parse_from_mf(), handles the whole format, and pays for it with a Boolean formula tree per transition. The automata Mata supports mostly come as plain source symbol target lines, which this reader tokenizes in place, without any intermediate representation.
