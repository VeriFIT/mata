Detailed Description

template<typename D>
class mata::posts::DeltaBuilder< D >

The generic transition relation: a vector of posts indexed by source state.

Builds a relation in its final form from transitions given in order: sources, then keys of a source, then targets of a key, each strictly increasing.

A vector of posts indexed by source state, over a chain of posts ending in a set of targets. The depth is deduced from P as key_arity. Posts are named by key index, not by container count: Key<0> is the topmost symbol, and PostAt<key_arity> is the innermost post.

Template Parameters
PThe post reached from one source state, itself parameterised down to the targets.
TTWhat one transition of this relation looks like and how to build one: a traits with a Type and a make(). Defaults to the symbol-named triple at arity 1 and to the tuple-keyed transition above it.
See also
mata::posts::DefaultTransitionTraits.
mata::DeltaLike. Builds a relation in its final form; the friendship below is its only back door.
DeltaBuilder.

Every post entry is created once with exact capacity, instead of being grown target by target. Arity 1 only: at a deeper key chain, the "targets" of a key are themselves keyed posts, which this builder does not shape.

Template Parameters
DThe concrete relation to build. Needs no extra members on D: the finished posts are installed through the DeltaBase it derives from.
