Class mata::posts::PostMoves¶

template<typename P>
class PostMoves¶

The moves of a post as Move instances: every (symbol, target) pair, or a range of them.

Available only at key_arity 1, since a Move holds a single key. Above that, use for_each_move().

Template Parameters:

P – The post visited.

Pre:

No symbol post in the range is without targets: for speed, the iterator does not skip one. Mata’s own writes keep a post so, as remove() and defragment() erase the symbol posts they empty. A symbol post created empty through mutable_state_post() has to get its targets before its moves are iterated. for_each_move() and the successor cursors have no such precondition.
