Class mata::posts::PostEntry¶

template<typename K, typename N>
class PostEntry : public mata::posts::internal::PostEntryCore<K, N>¶

One entry of a post: a single key, and the post nested under it.

When N is a mata::posts::TargetSetLike (an entry of a relation of key_arity 1), the entry also names its key symbol and its targets targets, following the NFA’s naming, and supports structured bindings: auto& [symbol, targets] = entry.

Template Parameters:

  • K – What this entry is keyed by.

  • N – The post nested under that key: another post, or a mata::posts::TargetSetLike at the bottom.
