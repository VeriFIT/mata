Detailed Description

A concept for a post, a range of entries that can be iterated, updated, and queried.

Note
Moving a post must leave its entries where they are, as moving a std::vector does, and must not throw. A relation moves its posts whenever it grows, and the targets passed to its add() may be borrowed from one of them. A std::vector that grows moves its elements only if that cannot throw, and copies them otherwise, which would leave such borrowed targets dangling.
