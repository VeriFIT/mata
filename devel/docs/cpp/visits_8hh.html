Detailed Description

The visits over a chain of posts: reading, writing and rebuilding at any nesting depth.

Free functions over a post P, each recursing on mata::arity_of until it reaches the targets. They are what mata::posts::Post, mata::posts::DeltaBase and mata::AutomatonBase share, so the depth is handled once, here, and not again in every class that holds a post.
