Lay the levels of appended_num_of_states states, taken from appended, after the levels of the first num_of_states states in levels, as the states of an operand shifted by num_of_states.

A level vector may miss states past the delta (they are on DEFAULT_LEVEL) or hold surplus entries, so both vectors are cut or padded to their state counts first; appending them as they are misplaces the shifted levels. appended may be levels itself.

Throw std::invalid_argument when the level metadata of nft is incomplete or out of range.

Operations that interpret transitions through the level vector (jump unwinding, inclusion, equivalence) must not silently treat a transducer with missing or malformed levels as a flat NFA; that produced wrong answers without any error (#791).
