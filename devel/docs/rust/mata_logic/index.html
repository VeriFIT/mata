Crate mata_logic 

Source
Expand description

Decision procedures for logics whose models are encoded as words, on top of mata_lazy.

A Logic builds formulas out of atoms, which are automata over bits, with ∧, ∨, ¬, ∃ and ∀, and decides them lazily: a formula is a mata_lazy::Formula term with one tape per free variable, and a query explores only what its answer needs. The encoding, and so the logic, is chosen by the atoms and the Padding: natural numbers or finite sets (WS1S) pad with zeros, integers in two’s complement (Presburger arithmetic, LIA) repeat the sign.

The front-end ws1s parses and decides formulas of WS1S. This crate is pure Rust. mata re-exports it as mata::logic.
