Decision procedures for logics whose models are encoded as words: the mata-logic crate, on top of lazy.

Everything of mata-logic is here; see Logic to start, or ws1s to decide WS1S formulas from text.

Modules§

ws1s
WS1S, the weak monadic second-order logic of one successor: formulas over positions and finite sets of positions, parsed from a MONA-like syntax and decided by a Logic with Padding::Zero.

Structs§

Logic
Builds formulas over variables from atoms given as automata, and decides them with mata_lazy.
Model
An assignment, as the bits of one encoding of each variable’s value, in the order they were read.
Prop
A formula of a Logic: a Term of its Formula with one tape per free variable, in the order of vars.
Var
A variable of a Logic.
