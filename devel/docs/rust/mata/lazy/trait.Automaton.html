Expand description

A finite automaton over tuples of symbols, the interface through which a formula reads its leaves.

A move reads one Symbol on every tape at once (lock step). EPSILON is padding, where the tape reads nothing while the other tapes read. ANY reads any one symbol of the tape’s alphabet. A move that pads every tape of an automaton with at least one tape is silent, like an epsilon transition of an NFA.

leaf copies the part reachable from the initial states, so that part must be finite.

fn alphabet(&self, tape: usize) -> impl IntoIterator<Item = Symbol>

Symbols of tape besides those that its moves read, as the alphabets of leaf_over, which adds its own: a complement over the leaf, and an ANY move on tape, range over them too. None by default.
